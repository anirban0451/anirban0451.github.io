I am an postdoctoral scholar at the Department of Public Health Sciences and Hollings Cancer Center in the Medical University of South Carolina (MUSC). My postdoctoral advisor is <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=8NeELr8AAAAJ&hl=en" style="color:skyblue;"> Prof. Souvik Seal</a>. At MUSC, I am working on statistical methods for biomarker discovery in single cell and spatial omics data. I received Ph.D. in Statistics in 2024 from the Department of Statistics and Data Science, 
Texas A&amp M University, working with <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=vqW0UqUAAAAJ&hl=en" style="color:skyblue;"> Prof. Matthias Katzfuss </a> on scalable statistical modeling of large spatial data. I hold a Masters in Statistics (2018) degree from Indian Statistical Institue, Kolkata.

My research interests include Gaussian processes, Bayesian inference, spatiotemporal statistics, factor modeling and their application in important scientific topics such as single cell and spatial omics analysis, environmental data modeling etc. I am also acitvely collaborating with colleagues from other universities. Feel free to contact if you would like to collaborate with me.
